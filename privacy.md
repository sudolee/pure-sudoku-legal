# Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: 2026-11-23*

This policy covers Pure Sudoku: Variants & Coach, published by an individual developer.

### 1. Current version
Pure Sudoku: Variants & Coach is a puzzle game. You do not create an account with us. Puzzles, hints, and lessons run on your device.

- **No account.** You do not sign in. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifier.
- **No ads or analytics.** This version does not include advertising, analytics, or attribution SDKs.
- **On this device.** When you finish a puzzle, the app saves a record: date, variant, grid size, difficulty, time, mistakes, hints used, the puzzle template and seed, and, for a challenge, its kind, code, and date. Achievements, theme, language, the hint switch, which charts you show, tutorial progress, and the times the app asked you to rate it stay on the device. The board you are still playing is not written into that history. A random id for this install is stored on the device so your Apple devices can tell their edits apart during iCloud sync. It is not an advertising id.
- **iCloud sync, Apple only.** On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, settings (theme, language, and the hint switch), finished-game records, and achievements can sync through your iCloud account, in that account’s private CloudKit database. The copy is available only to Apple devices signed in with the same account. We do not receive a copy on a server we operate, and we cannot read data kept in your private iCloud storage. Sync is on when iCloud is available. You can turn it off in Settings. Turning it off stops upload and download on that device. Records already in iCloud stay there until you delete that iCloud data from the device’s iCloud storage settings. Android does not sync.
- **Device backup.** If you turn on iCloud Backup, or you back this device up to a computer, the backup can include the app’s local data. We cannot read those backups. Removing the app deletes the copy stored in the app. A backup made earlier can still hold a copy until you delete that backup.
- **Purchases.** Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase on the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google charges your store account. We never see your card number. To tell whether this device has Pro, the app asks RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id created on the device, the store receipt or purchase token, and the technical data needed to make that request, such as platform, app version, locale, and IP address. It does not receive your game records or achievements. If RevenueCat’s primary address is blocked, the same check may go to RevenueCat’s backup host, `api.rc-backup.com`. Their policy is at [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). If you bought this app as a paid download before it became free, when it was named Pure Sudoku: 9 Logic Puzzles, that Apple receipt keeps Pro. The check uses the original download version already in the receipt.
- **Sharing and Photos.** When you share a result or save a challenge card, the image is made on the device. Sharing hands that image to the app you pick in the system share sheet. Saving asks for permission to add the image to your photo library. We do not upload it, and we do not read the rest of your library.
- **Ratings.** A rating request opens the App Store or Google Play. The review goes to the store. We store only the times we asked, on the device, so we do not ask too often.

### 2. What we do not keep
We do not run an account system or a game server. Puzzle templates ship inside the app. In ordinary use, data leaves the device only for a purchase check, an iCloud sync you have not turned off, a share or photo save you start, a rating page you open, or a device backup you have turned on.

### 3. Children
Pure Sudoku: Variants & Coach is a puzzle game for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no chat, social network, or public profile.

### 4. Changes
We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change describes a new way your game data leaves the device, we will update this policy before that version is released.
