# Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: 2026-11-26*

This policy covers Pure Sudoku: Variants & Coach, published by an individual developer.

### 1. Current version
Pure Sudoku: Variants & Coach is a puzzle game. You do not create an account with us. Puzzles, hints, and lessons run on your device.

- **No developer account.** You do not sign up with us. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising identifier.
- **No ads or third-party analytics.** This version does not include advertising, attribution, or third-party analytics SDKs.
- **On this device.** When you finish a puzzle, the app saves a record: date, variant, grid size, difficulty, time, mistakes, hints used, score and scoring rule version, whether the run counted as ranked, the puzzle template and seed, and, for a challenge, its kind, code, and date. Achievements, theme, language, the hint switch, daily reminder preference, which charts you show, tutorial progress, cached scoring parameters, pending leaderboard submissions, external Pro challenge trial counts, and the times the app asked you to rate it stay on the device. The board you are still playing is not written into that history. A random id for this install is stored on the device so your Apple devices can tell their edits apart during iCloud sync. It is not an advertising id.
- **Home Screen & Desktop Widgets.** On supported devices, the app writes a small local summary of today’s daily challenge and streak (via Apple App Groups on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS, or local widget storage on Android) so the widget can display it. The widget does not upload data.
- **Local daily reminders.** If you turn on daily challenge reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification server is used for reminders.
- **iCloud sync, Apple only.** On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, settings (theme, language, and the hint switch), finished-game records, and achievements can sync through your iCloud account, in that account’s private CloudKit database. The copy is available only to Apple devices signed in with the same account. We do not receive a copy on a server we operate, and we cannot read data kept in your private iCloud storage. Sync is on when iCloud is available. You can turn it off in Settings. Turning it off stops upload and download on that device. Records already in iCloud stay there until you delete that iCloud data from the device’s iCloud storage settings. Android does not sync.
- **Apple Game Center leaderboards, Apple only.** On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, you may optionally sign in to Apple Game Center to view and join Global and Friends leaderboards. When signed in, the app submits your ranked scores along with compact non-personal game metadata (difficulty, solve duration, mistake/hint counts, or contributing game counts) to Apple Game Center, and fetches leaderboard entries (rank, score, display name, alias, and small Game Center avatar images cached locally on your device). Viewing the Friends leaderboard asks for system permission to access your Game Center friends list. We do not collect, copy, or store your Game Center profile or friends list on any server we run. Game Center is operated by Apple under [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Anonymous difficulty calibration, Apple only.** To keep target completion times fair across puzzle modes without running our own server, the app fetches a public scoring configuration from Apple’s CloudKit Public Database and, for a sampled share of completed, unassisted (0-hint), non-replay puzzles, submits an anonymous solve-time sample (`modeKey`, duration in seconds, mistake count, scoring version, daily flag, and ISO week). These samples contain no player id, device id, name, or other personal information.
- **Challenge links, QR codes, and clipboard.** Sharing a challenge link or QR code encodes only the puzzle parameters (variant, grid size, difficulty, template index, and seed)—no personal data. When you open or return to the app, it checks plain text on your clipboard locally on the device solely to detect a challenge code or challenge link so it can offer to open that puzzle; clipboard text is never stored or uploaded.
- **Device backup.** If you turn on iCloud Backup, or you back this device up to a computer, the backup can include the app’s local data. We cannot read those backups. Removing the app deletes the copy stored in the app. A backup made earlier can still hold a copy until you delete that backup.
- **Purchases.** Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase on the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google charges your store account. We never see your card number. To tell whether this device has Pro, the app asks RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id created on the device, the store receipt or purchase token, and the technical data needed to make that request, such as platform, app version, locale, and IP address. It does not receive your game records or achievements. If RevenueCat’s primary address is blocked, the same check may go to RevenueCat’s backup host, `api.rc-backup.com`. Their policy is at [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). If you bought this app as a paid download before it became free, when it was named Pure Sudoku: 9 Logic Puzzles, that Apple receipt keeps Pro. The check uses the original download version already in the receipt.
- **Sharing and Photos.** When you share a result or save a challenge card, the image is made on the device. Sharing hands that image or challenge link to the app you pick in the system share sheet. Saving asks for permission to add the image to your photo library. We do not upload it, and we do not read the rest of your library.
- **Ratings.** A rating prompt uses the native App Store or Google Play review dialog or opens the store page. The review goes directly to the store. We store only the times we asked, on the device, so we do not ask too often.

### 2. What we do not keep
We do not run an account system or a game server. Puzzle templates ship inside the app. In ordinary use, data leaves the device only for a purchase check, an iCloud sync you have not turned off, optional Apple Game Center leaderboards, anonymous CloudKit Public Database scoring calibration samples and config fetches, a share or photo save you start, a store rating you submit, or a device backup you have turned on.

### 3. Children
Pure Sudoku: Variants & Coach is a puzzle game for a general audience. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no chat or social feed. Optional Game Center leaderboards on Apple devices follow Apple’s Game Center age and parental controls.

### 4. Changes
We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change describes a new way your game data leaves the device, we will update this policy before that version is released.
